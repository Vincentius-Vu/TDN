## PHẦN A. TỪ VỰNG TRỌNG TÂM (CORE VOCABULARY)
Đề thi khảo sát năng lực thường sử dụng các từ vựng thuộc các chủ đề dưới đây. Học sinh cần hiểu nghĩa tiếng Anh và biết cách dùng trong câu.

### 1. Chủ đề Science & Environment (Khoa học & Môi trường)
- **Renewable energy** (n): Năng lượng tái tạo. *Ex: Solar power is a renewable energy source.*
- **Pollution** (n): Sự ô nhiễm. *Ex: Air pollution is bad for our health.*
- **Habitat** (n): Môi trường sống. *Ex: Forests are the natural habitat of many animals.*
- **Evaporation** (n): Sự bay hơi. *Ex: The sun causes the evaporation of water from lakes.*
- **Gravity** (n): Trọng lực. *Ex: Gravity pulls objects toward the center of the Earth.*
- **Photosynthesis** (n): Sự quang hợp. *Ex: Plants make their own food through photosynthesis.*

### 2. Chủ đề Society & History (Xã hội & Lịch sử)
- **Ancestor** (n): Tổ tiên. *Ex: Our ancestors built this ancient temple.*
- **Agriculture** (n): Nông nghiệp. *Ex: Agriculture is an important part of our country's economy.*
- **Citizen** (n): Công dân. *Ex: Every citizen must obey the law.*
- **Independence** (n): Sự độc lập. *Ex: Vietnam gained its independence in 1945.*

**Hướng dẫn:** 
- Đọc lướt (Skimming) để nắm ý chính toàn đoạn.
- Đọc quét (Scanning) để tìm từ khóa (keywords) trả lời câu hỏi chi tiết.

**Passage 1:**
Water is essential for life on Earth. About 71% of the Earth's surface is water-covered, and the oceans hold about 96.5% of all Earth's water. Water also exists in the air as water vapor, in rivers and lakes, in icecaps and glaciers. The water cycle describes how water evaporates from the surface of the earth, rises into the atmosphere, cools and condenses into rain or snow in clouds, and falls again to the surface as precipitation.

*Questions:*
1. How much of the Earth's surface is covered by water?
   A. 71%
   B. 96.5%
   C. 100%
   D. 29%
2. According to the passage, which holds the most water on Earth?
   A. Glaciers
   B. Rivers
   C. Oceans
   D. Atmosphere

   A. Precipitation
   B. Evaporation
   C. Condensation
   D. Freezing

1. A (About 71% of the Earth's surface is water-covered)
2. C (the oceans hold about 96.5% of all Earth's water)
3. B (evaporates from the surface)

## PHẦN D. VIẾT ĐOẠN VĂN (PARAGRAPH WRITING)

**Đề bài mẫu:** 
Write a short paragraph (50 - 80 words) about the importance of reading books.

**Hướng dẫn viết (Outline):**
- **Topic sentence (Câu chủ đề):** Khẳng định việc đọc sách rất quan trọng. *(Reading books is very important for several reasons.)*
- **Body sentences (Các ý phát triển):** 
  - Idea 1: Đọc sách giúp mở rộng kiến thức. *(Firstly, it helps us expand our knowledge about the world.)*
  - Idea 2: Đọc sách giúp cải thiện vốn từ vựng và sự tập trung. *(Secondly, reading improves our vocabulary and concentration.)*
  - Idea 3: Nó cũng là cách giải trí tốt. *(Finally, it is a great way to relax and reduce stress after a long day of studying.)*
- **Concluding sentence (Câu kết luận):** Tóm lại, đọc sách mang lại nhiều lợi ích. *(In conclusion, reading is a wonderful habit that everyone should have.)*

**Sample Essay (Bài mẫu):**
Reading books is very important for several reasons. Firstly, it helps us expand our knowledge about the world, from science to history. Secondly, reading regularly improves our vocabulary and makes our brain work faster. Furthermore, reading a good book is a great way to relax and reduce stress after a long day at school. In conclusion, reading is a wonderful habit that brings many benefits to our lives, and everyone should try to read every day. (77 words)

*Lưu ý cho phụ huynh:* Khi chấm bài viết tự luận tiếng Anh, hãy chú ý đến cấu trúc ngữ pháp (dùng đúng thì), lỗi chính tả, và logic kết nối giữa các câu (First, Second, Furthermore...). Không cần dùng từ vựng quá phức tạp.

## PHẦN E. LUYỆN TẬP MỞ RỘNG (ADVANCED PRACTICE)

### 1. Trắc nghiệm Từ vựng & Ngữ pháp (Vocabulary & Grammar MCQ)
*Khoanh tròn vào đáp án đúng nhất.*

**Question 1:** The ____ of the new hospital will help many people in the area.
A. construction   B. agriculture   C. evaporation   D. environment
*(Đáp án: A. construction - việc xây dựng)*

**Question 2:** We should use ____ bags instead of plastic ones to protect the environment.
A. single-use     B. reusable      C. throwaway     D. non-recyclable
*(Đáp án: B. reusable - có thể tái sử dụng)*

**Question 3:** Water ____ at 100 degrees Celsius.
A. boils          B. is boiling    C. boiled        D. boil
*(Đáp án: A. boils - sự thật hiển nhiên dùng thì hiện tại đơn)*

**Question 4:** If you mix blue and yellow, you ____ green.
A. are getting    B. got           C. will get      D. get
*(Đáp án: D. get - câu điều kiện loại 0)*

**Question 5:** The sun is the main source of ____ for all living things on Earth.
A. oxygen         B. energy        C. soil          D. gravity
*(Đáp án: B. energy - năng lượng)*

**Question 6:** Dinosaurs became ____ millions of years ago.
A. extinct        B. endangered    C. active        D. rare
*(Đáp án: A. extinct - tuyệt chủng)*

**Question 7:** My mother asked me ____ the window because it was raining.
A. to close       B. closing       C. closed        D. close
*(Đáp án: A. to close - ask someone to do something)*

**Question 8:** Astronauts travel into ____ to study other planets.
A. atmosphere     B. sky           C. space         D. gravity
*(Đáp án: C. space - vũ trụ)*

**Question 9:** Plants need sunlight, water, and ____ dioxide to make their food.
A. oxygen         B. carbon        C. nitrogen      D. hydrogen
*(Đáp án: B. carbon dioxide - khí CO2)*

**Question 10:** She hasn't finished her homework ____.
A. already        B. yet           C. just          D. still
*(Đáp án: B. yet - dùng trong câu phủ định thì hiện tại hoàn thành)*

### 2. Viết lại câu mở rộng (Advanced Sentence Transformation)
1. This is the first time I have seen such a beautiful animal.
   -> I have never _________________________________________.
   *(Đáp án: I have never seen such a beautiful animal before.)*
2. Although he was tired, he finished his project.
   -> In spite of _________________________________________.
   *(Đáp án: In spite of being tired, he finished his project.)*
3. People say that elephants are very smart.
   -> Elephants are said ____________________________________.
   *(Đáp án: Elephants are said to be very smart.)*
4. "Where did you go yesterday?" John asked Mary.
   -> John asked Mary _____________________________________.
   *(Đáp án: John asked Mary where she had gone the day before.)*

### 3. Đọc hiểu nâng cao (Advanced Reading)
**Passage 2:**
Forests cover about 30% of the Earth's land surface. They are home to many different kinds of plants and animals. Trees in the forest are very important because they produce oxygen, which we need to breathe. They also absorb carbon dioxide, a gas that causes global warming. However, every year, a large area of forests is destroyed because people cut down trees for wood and to clear land for farming. This process is called deforestation. Deforestation can lead to many problems, such as climate change and the loss of animal habitats.

*Questions:*
1. What percentage of the Earth's land is covered by forests?
   A. 70%      B. 30%      C. 100%     D. 50%
2. Why are trees important?
   A. They cause global warming.
   B. They clear land for farming.
   C. They produce oxygen and absorb carbon dioxide.
   D. They destroy animal habitats.
3. What is "deforestation"?
   A. Planting more trees.
   B. The process of cutting down trees.
   C. Protecting animal habitats.
   D. The production of oxygen.

*Answers:*
1. B
2. C
3. B
